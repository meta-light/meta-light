# Templates

Post-by-post shapes for each type, with examples. The examples come from past published threads, so the numbers are dated (mid-2025). Use them for shape and tone, never as facts.

## Report thread (5 to 10 posts)

Promotes a published report or flash note. Usually posted from the author's account. Sponsored pieces go out from the publication's account with "Unlocked by @[sponsor]" in the first and last post.

```
1/ [Hook: the single most interesting finding, with a number if it fits]

2/ [What it is: the mechanism in one or two sentences]

3/ [Why it matters or why now: market size, catalyst, timing]

4/ [Traction: three or four bulleted metrics, each with context]

5/ [Differentiation or competitive position]

6/ What to watch:

- Risks: [specific]

- Catalysts: [specific, with timing]

7/ [Forward-looking takeaway: what happens if the thesis holds]

Full report: [LINK]
```

Choose five to seven points from the report. Skip sections that don't make a strong post.


## Insight thread (5 to 8 posts)

A timely take not tied to a report. Ends on the takeaway, not a pitch. Tag the protocol and founders when the handles are verified.

```
1/ [Hook: the timely insight or news angle]

2/ [Context or mechanism]

3/ [Key data point]

4/ [Comparison against peers or the status quo]

5/ [Why it matters]

6/ [What to watch]

7/ Net-net: [one or two sentence takeaway]
```

Good topics: new protocol launches with novel mechanics, sharp moves in TVL, fees or usage, governance votes that change economics, head-to-head comparisons.

### Example

```
1/ TapeDrive is taking an unusual approach to onchain storage on Solana.

After winning Grand Champion at @colosseum's latest hackathon, it's live on devnet with mainnet next.

2/ TapeDrive plugs into Solana's validators for security and speed, with no side-chain or extra consensus layer.

Writers pack data into onchain "tapes", pay once, and miners store it permanently under a proof-of-access scheme.

Pricing is ~1,400x cheaper than current Solana account rent.

3/ TIMD-001 overhauls the economics.

Every write pre-funds 100 years of storage and streams those fees to miners over time.

- ~1 minute blocks, one proof per miner

- Streak bonuses for operators who never miss a block

- Participant targets and difficulty retune every ~10 minutes

4/ TIMD-002 adds updatable tapes.

Append or edit data until it's final, then seal it permanently.

That fits compressed token accounts, staged mints and append-only logs.

5/ Net-net: TapeDrive cuts Solana storage costs by three orders of magnitude and ties miner rewards to real demand for storage.
```

## Single post

One observation, one number, one implication. Under 280 characters when possible.

```
[Observation with number and context].

[Implication in one sentence].
```

## Article (1,000 to 2,500 words)

Native long-form on X. Follows the research-writing report structure at a smaller scale, so read `../research-writing/references/reports.md` for section guidance. Use three to six visuals.

```
# [Title: specific, not clickbait]

[One or two paragraphs: the thesis up front]

## [Context: what it is and why now]

## [Core analysis: the argument with data, tables, charts]

## [Implications: what changes for users, builders or holders]

## [Risks: specific counterarguments and unknowns]

## Key takeaways

- [Three to five quantified bullets]

[Optional CTA: link to the full report or newsletter]
```

Article rules:

- Thesis in the first two paragraphs.
- Every section opens with an assertion and backs it with data.
- Comparison tables beat paragraphs of competitor descriptions.
- The risks section is required.
- The CTA is one line, at the end.
