Instruments by class

Spot buys a request now. Quantity is a fixed amount of tokens delivered later. Dollar is a claim on dollars of inference at list price. Access is a stake that admits a seller to a network. Discount is against retail; for Liquid, retail is the median OpenRouter endpoint for the same model.

Mercatus OSTR menu vs OpenRouter

$ per million tokens. Blend = 3:1 input:output. Peak and off-peak are the dearest and cheapest time-of-day tiers. The OpenRouter median is across that model's endpoints; the cheapest is often one promotional or quantized host.

Orbio CREDIT order book

Bars are dollars of credit offered at each discount. Each CREDIT redeems for $1 of Orbio inference, so an ask at 40% sells $1 of inference for $0.60.
